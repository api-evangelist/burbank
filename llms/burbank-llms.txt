# Burbank

> Burbank is a Cardiff and London based payments technology company building card-present authentication for online and remote payments. Its flagship Card Present Over Internet (CPoI) technology lets consumers authenticate anywhere using the globally familiar "tap and PIN" gesture, alongside a SoftPOS product and an Issuer Toolkit for identity verification. Burbank ships a white-label SDK to issuers, acquirers, and payment service providers to reduce online fraud and improve conversion.

Burbank does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI definition, or developer portal at this time. Integration is delivered as a white-label SDK (mobile library plus containerized backend services) under commercial engagement. Backed by Anthemis, Mouro Capital, and Portfolio Ventures.

## Solutions

- [Card Present Over Internet (CPoI)](https://www.burbankdev.co.uk/): Card-present authentication for online payments using tap and PIN.
- [SoftPOS](https://www.burbankdev.co.uk/): Software point-of-sale acceptance.
- [Issuer Toolkit](https://www.burbankdev.co.uk/): Identity verification for card issuers.
